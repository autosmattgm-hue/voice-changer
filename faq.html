Product and lead generation

What is MAT LEADS AI PRO X?

MAT LEADS AI PRO X is a Google Maps lead generation SaaS. It finds local businesses that need websites, SEO, and digital marketing, scores every opportunity from 0 to 100, and uses AI to write outreach messages, build client websites, and prepare priced proposals — all inside one workspace.

How does the MAT LEADS Google Maps phone number extraction work?

The Lead Engine searches Google Maps and OpenStreetMap business records for your chosen category and location. Each result includes the business's publicly listed phone number together with its name, category, address, rating, reviews, website, and Google Maps link. The phone number arrives as part of the lead record, ready for cold calls, WhatsApp outreach, or AI-written follow-up messages.

Where do the leads come from?

Business records are collected from official Google APIs and OpenStreetMap data. The platform then audits each lead's website for HTTPS, mobile viewport, SEO metadata, contact forms, social links, and business emails to rank the opportunity.

What is the Opportunity Score?

The Opportunity Score is a weighted 0-100 rating for every lead. It measures missing websites, weak SEO, missing SSL, slow pages, and conversion gaps, so you contact the highest-value businesses first instead of guessing.

What is Business AI Tycoon?

Business AI Tycoon is an AI negotiation coach. It tells you what to say, what price to quote, and how to win each deal, and it produces copy-paste WhatsApp scripts with a share button for fast client conversations.

Does MAT LEADS AI PRO X send outreach emails automatically?

No. The Outreach Generator writes cold emails, follow-up emails, redesign proposals, SEO proposals, and AI automation proposals for you. You review the text and send it from your own inbox, so every message stays under your control.

AI website builder

How does the AI website builder create a website?

On any lead, tick "Use AI to create website" and select Create Website. The AI returns a complete multi-page HTML website — hero, services, about, and contact sections with modern styling — in about 60 seconds. You then refine it with plain chat instructions such as "make the header darker" or "add a pricing section". Every finished site can be downloaded as HTML or shared via a public link.

Can I download or share a website built with MAT LEADS AI Website Studio?

Yes. Every AI-built website gets a public share link you can send to clients, plus a full HTML download that you can host anywhere — your own server, Netlify, GitHub Pages, or any static host.

Do I need design or coding skills to build a client website?

No. You pick a lead, click Create Website, and describe changes in plain language. The builder handles layout, colors, sections, and mobile responsiveness for you.
