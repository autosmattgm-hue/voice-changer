How phone lead extraction works

1. Search the area

Choose country, state, city, or ZIP code, then pick an industry and keyword — for example "plumbers in Austin".

2. Collect public records

The Lead Engine queries Google Maps and OpenStreetMap business records for that area and returns every matching business.

3. Enrich each lead

Each lead carries the publicly listed phone number, address, rating, reviews, website, and Google Maps link, plus a website audit.

4. Score and analyze

AI assigns an Opportunity Score from 0 to 100 and a short analysis with the next best action for the lead.

What each phone lead includes

Phone number

The phone number the business publicly lists on its Google Maps profile, ready for calls or WhatsApp outreach.

Business identity

Name, category, address, rating, and reviews.

Website and Maps link

Website URL and a direct Google Maps link for every lead.

Website audit

HTTPS, mobile viewport, SEO metadata, contact forms, social links, and business emails.

Opportunity Score

Weighted 0-100 scoring for missing websites, weak SEO, no SSL, slow pages, and conversion gaps.

AI analysis

A short AI summary of the lead with revenue forecast and ideal service package.

Phone lead generation FAQ

Where do the phone numbers come from?

Phone numbers come from the business listings publicly visible on Google Maps and OpenStreetMap — the same contact details the business publishes for its customers. The platform does not generate or guess numbers.

How many leads do I get per plan?

Starter includes 100 leads per month, Professional 1,000, Growth Plus 3,500, and Agency unlimited. The free trial returns up to 5 leads per search for 2 searches.

Can I contact leads directly from the platform?

You get the phone number, email when available, and Google Maps link for every lead, plus AI outreach scripts you copy into your own phone or inbox. Sending always stays under your control.
