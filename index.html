What is MAT LEADS AI PRO X?

MAT LEADS AI PRO X is a Google Maps lead generation SaaS. It finds local businesses that need websites, SEO, and digital marketing, scores every opportunity from 0 to 100, and uses AI to write outreach messages, build client websites, and prepare priced proposals — all inside one workspace.

How does the MAT LEADS Google Maps phone number extraction work?

The Lead Engine searches Google Maps and OpenStreetMap business records for your chosen category and location. Each result includes the business's publicly listed phone number together with its name, category, address, rating, reviews, website, and Google Maps link. The phone number arrives as part of the lead record, ready for cold calls, WhatsApp outreach, or AI-written follow-up messages.

How does the AI website builder create a website?

On any lead, tick "Use AI to create website" and select Create Website. The AI returns a complete multi-page HTML website — hero, services, about, and contact sections with modern styling — in about 60 seconds. You then refine it with plain chat instructions such as "make the header darker" or "add a pricing section". Every finished site can be downloaded as HTML or shared via a public link.
